How to verify ATAMO

Users should rely on official public references and independent blockchain verification rather than screenshots, social posts, or copied addresses.

1. Verify the domain Confirm that you are using the official ATAMO website and not a look-alike domain.
2. Verify the contracts Compare the published addresses on the contracts page with verified blockchain records.
3. Verify the source reference Match the deployment against the official repository, release notes, and public documentation.
